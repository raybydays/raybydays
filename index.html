How a day becomes an entry.

Four things happen on every stop, in roughly this order. The writing is always last, and always late.

{ Hour 01. }#. 01

Arrive with nothing booked

A bed for two nights and no plan past breakfast. Everything good on this site happened in the gap after that.

{ Day 01–02. }#. 02

Same route three mornings running. You start noticing which bakery opens first and who queues where.

{ Day 02–04. }#. 03

Eat the thing twice

Once to try it, once to be sure. Most of the camera roll is food and nobody has complained yet.

Trains, airports, the night bus. Distance makes it clearer what actually mattered that week.
